Cross-chain bridge fees, explained

By Mara Voss Sep 4, 2026 1 min read

Moving assets across chains still confuses most people, and the fee you pay is rarely the fee you were quoted. Three things decide the real cost: the route the aggregator picks, gas on the destination chain, and how deep liquidity is at that moment.

Where the cost actually goes

On a typical transfer you pay a source-chain gas fee, a bridge or liquidity fee, and destination gas. A routing aggregator like LiFi Bridge compares paths and picks the cheapest total instead of locking you into a single pool.

For background, the Ethereum docs on bridges are a solid primer.

How to pay less

  • Bridge larger amounts less often — fixed costs dominate small transfers.
  • Check settlement time and slippage before you sign.
  • Prefer routes with native destination liquidity.

A small delay for a better rate is almost always worth it.
