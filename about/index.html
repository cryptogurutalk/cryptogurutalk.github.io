About Crosscurrent

Crosscurrent covers the infrastructure that moves tokens and messages between blockchains. Its reporting focuses on bridge design, validator and relayer changes, liquidity conditions, security incidents, and the practical effects of protocol upgrades.

Editor Mara Velez has spent eight years covering blockchain infrastructure, first as a technical reporter for open-source developer publications and later reporting on cross-chain systems for digital-asset research teams. She reads technical documentation, tracks incident timelines, and asks protocol teams for specifics when the public record is incomplete.

The publication favors clear reporting over token hype. Crosscurrent is independently operated, and its coverage is guided by evidence, useful context, and the questions bridge users ask before moving funds.
