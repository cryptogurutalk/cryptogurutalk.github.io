About Northbound

Northbound is an independent publication about crypto bridges: the contracts, validators, liquidity routes, exploits, audits, and governance decisions that connect otherwise separate chains. Coverage stays close to the mechanics, with plain-English context for people moving assets and technical detail for readers building protocols.

Editorial direction comes from Mara Voss, editor and former infrastructure reporter who spent eight years covering wallet security, interoperability standards, and incident response for developer publications. She reviews primary announcements, audit reports, governance records, and on-chain evidence before assigning a story; corrections are noted openly.
